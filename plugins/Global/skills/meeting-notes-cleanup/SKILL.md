# Meeting Notes Cleanup

Turn rough meeting notes into a concise, readable record.

- Group discussion by topic and separate decisions, actions, open questions, and context.
- Preserve names, dates, commitments, and uncertainty; mark missing owners or deadlines unspecified.
- Do not add decisions or commitments absent from the notes.
