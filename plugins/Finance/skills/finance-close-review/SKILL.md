# Finance Close Review

Review a month-end close package for completeness and explain material variances.

- Check the supplied close checklist and mark items complete, missing, or unclear.
- Compare current results with the supplied prior period, budget, or forecast; summarize material variances.
- Cite source figures. Flag assumptions and questions; do not invent policy or sign off on the close.
